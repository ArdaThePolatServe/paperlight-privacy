Privacy Policy

Paperlight for iOS · Last updated 25 October 2026

Paperlight has no server, no account and no sign-in. Your scans are created on your iPhone, stored on your iPhone, and read only by your iPhone.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains why it is true, specifically enough that you can check it rather than take our word for it.

The short version

  • We collect no data. Not your documents, not their contents, not how you use the app, not an identifier of any kind.
  • There is no account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Paperlight does not send your scans anywhere.
  • We cannot see your documents, because they never reach us. There is no "us" to reach — no server exists.

Where your scans go

When you scan or import a page, Paperlight writes the image and the resulting PDF into the app's own storage on your device. That storage is included in your device backup, encrypted with your device passcode, and removed if you delete the app.

Text recognition and summarisation are worth singling out, because in most apps they are the moment your document gets uploaded. In Paperlight they are not: both run through Apple frameworks that execute on the device itself. Your documents are never transmitted for processing.

Permissions, and why each one is asked for

  • Camera — to photograph pages. Requested the first time you tap Scan, after a screen explaining why. Paperlight does not use the camera at any other time.
  • Photo Library — to import images you pick. Paperlight reads only the items you select and never browses your library. The app works fully without it.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — optional, off by default, used only to unlock the app or an individual document. Authentication is performed by iOS; Paperlight receives a yes or no and never sees your biometric data, which never leaves the Secure Enclave.

You can withdraw any of these at any time in iOS Settings. Paperlight will tell you what stopped working rather than failing silently.

Purchases

Paperlight Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Your payment details go to Apple and are never seen by Paperlight — the app receives only a signed receipt saying whether a purchase is active.

There is no third-party subscription or analytics vendor in the app. Nothing about your purchase is shared with anyone other than Apple, who handle it under their own privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

Paperlight contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK that transmits anything. We do not know how many documents you have scanned, which features you use, or whether you have opened the app today.

The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, because there is nothing it could ask to track you for.

iCloud

Paperlight does not sync your documents at present. If a future version offers iCloud sync, it will store your scans in your own iCloud account under your Apple Account — not on any server of ours, and not somewhere we can read. That would remain true by construction: we would still have no server to receive it.

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud, your Paperlight documents are included in that backup, as with any app. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Paperlight is a document scanner with no social features, no user-generated content shared with anyone, no advertising and no data collection. It is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal data a company holds about you.

Paperlight holds none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase — a request would return an empty result. Your data is in your hands already:

  • Export — share or save any document as a PDF or image at any time.
  • Delete — delete documents in the app; the Trash holds them for 30 days and then removes them permanently. Deleting the app removes everything at once.

Changes to this policy

If Paperlight ever changes what it does with your data, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change. We will not quietly start collecting something and document it later.
